# Project-Lion-Course

Hi! Project LionCourse is a website where students can search for other students who have taken certain courses. Students list the courses they’ve taken and how to reach them. 

Have you ever wanted to take a Columbia course, only to find that there is no information on it on CULPA or the Vergil course evaluations? Or perhaps you find a review, but you have more questions, and since the author is anonymous, you can’t ask?

This project aims to supplement existing solutions (asking on Reddit or in major-specific channels) by lowering the barrier for students to share information about courses with each other. 

Signing up takes 5 minutes: you list whichever courses you have taken and are willing to talk about, as well as your preferred contact method. For example:

John Doe
abcd1234@columbia.edu 
COMS4444
IEOR3700
MUSI3333

Once you are in: You can search for a course that you want to ask about, and students who have taken that course will show up in the directory, and you can contact them to ask your questions. No reply is guaranteed, but at least you know who you can reach out to. For example:

“COMS4444”
Alice Brown
Bob Smith
Charlie Winters

Privacy is taken seriously: At any time, you can remove your profile or opt out of being contacted. The only other people who have access to the directory are other Columbia students, and the information cannot be accessed by web-scrappers. Any login-information is used only for the purposes of logging in and stored securely under modern cryptographic standards using salts and hashing. 

Feel free to contact ming.wei@columbia.edu if you have any feedback or questions!
