Ask someone who took the class.

CULPA and Vergil evaluations are great when a course has reviews. When it doesn't, or when you have a follow-up question for an anonymous reviewer, there's nobody to ask.

LionCourse is a directory of Columbia students who are willing to talk about courses they've taken. Search a course code and you get a list of people you can contact.

COMS4444 → Alice Brown, Bob Smith, Charlie Winters

Adding yourself takes a few minutes: your name, how to reach you, and the courses you're happy to answer questions about. You don't have to use your real name or school email, and you can hide your listing whenever you want.

Only @columbia.edu accounts can sign in or see the directory.
