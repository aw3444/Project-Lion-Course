LionCourse

Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

LionCourse is a student project, not an official Columbia University service. This page explains exactly what it stores and who can see it.

What is stored

  • When you sign in: your Columbia email address, the name on your Google account, and an account ID. This is what lets you log in. Your password is never seen or stored by LionCourse; you type it into Columbia's and Google's own login pages.
  • If you save a listing: the name, contact information, and courses you enter, whether your listing is shown in search, and when you last updated it.

LionCourse does not use ads or tracking cookies. The site runs on Google Firebase, and Google may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) when pages are loaded.

Who can see it

  • Your listing is visible only to people signed in with a @columbia.edu account, and only while "Show me in search results" is checked.
  • A hidden listing is visible only to you.
  • The person who runs LionCourse can see all stored information through the Firebase admin console, and uses it only to keep the site working and deal with abuse reports.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Anything in a visible listing can be read by other Columbia students, who could copy it. Only put contact information there that you're comfortable sharing with them.

Removing your information

  • Delete my listing removes your name, contact, and courses right away.
  • Your sign-in record (email and account ID) stays so you can log in again. To have it removed too, email ming.wei@columbia.edu.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
