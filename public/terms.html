Terms of use

Last updated November 24, 2026

LionCourse is a student project, not an official Columbia University service. By using it, you agree to the following.

Who can use it

Current Columbia students with a @columbia.edu account.

Your listing

  • Use a real name or a nickname, your choice, and contact information you actually check.
  • Only list courses you've taken.
  • Don't create listings for other people.

Contacting people

  • Only contact people to ask about courses they've listed.
  • Don't spam, harass, or advertise to anyone you find here.
  • Don't copy the directory or collect people's contact information in bulk.
  • No one is obligated to reply.

Removal

Listings or accounts that break these rules may be hidden or removed without notice. You can hide your own listing at any time, or email to have it deleted.

No guarantees

LionCourse is provided as is. It may be unavailable, change, or shut down. Advice you get from other students is their own opinion, not Columbia's or LionCourse's.
